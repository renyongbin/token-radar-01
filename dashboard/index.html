How the radar scores

Each protocol gets a score from 0 to 1, then an implied value. The score is five parts of the snapshot, nothing else:

  • Size, 45%. log10 of TVL, stretched between the snapshot’s $5M floor and the largest eligible TVL. More value locked, more to share if a token ever issues.
  • Growth, 25%. The 7-day TVL change, winsorised to −30%…+50%. Users arriving now count; users leaving count against.
  • Age, 15%. Years since first listed, up to the snapshot date, capped at 4 years. A protocol that has run for years at scale with no token on record has the largest established base to reward.
  • Chains, 10%. How many chains it runs on, capped at 12. More chains, more places to farm it.
  • Sector, 5%. A small prior for the category: fee-earning venues score a touch higher than pure infrastructure. It never decides a rank on its own.

Implied value = TVL × (2% + 48% × score²). A middling score earns a middling fraction of TVL; only a strong score earns a large one. Ranks follow the value, highest first. A missing 7-day change or listing date scores neutral (0.5) on that part, never zero.

Eligibility: a protocol records no token (both its token id and symbol are empty in the snapshot) and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades, not a centralised exchange and not a bridge or liquid-staking product. The board holds all 40 watchlist protocols, the 158 other eligible protocols, and 2 fillers — pre-token protocols kept out of the watchlist only by a version marker in the name, flagged on their rows. The board says a protocol records no token, never that it has none: the record is not proof.

Implied values are this entry’s own claim, computed from the snapshot. They are not a price, not a prediction that a token will launch, and not financial advice.
